# TAP Student, Anh Tang, won best poster award at SITE Conference

<Photo src={site_Spring25Cover.src} caption="Anh Tang, shines at SITE Conference"/> 

TAP student, Anh Tang, won the [best poster award](https://www.learntechlib.org/awards/SITE/2025/) at the 36th annual conference of the 
[Society for Information Technology and Teacher Education](https://site.aace.org/conf/) (SITE 2025) in Orlando, Florida. Her project was titled 
["Incorporating Gamification Elements in Interactive Programming Workshops"](https://www.learntechlib.org/primary/p/225671).
