# Third Place in the Student Research Contest at CCSC:SE 2026

TAP participated in the [2026 Annual CCSC:Southeastern Conference 
(CCSC:SE 2026)](https://ccsc.org/southeastern/conference.php?year=2026) 
that took place during October 23-24, 2026 at East Tennessee State University in Johnson City, Tennessee.

<Photo src={awardPhoto.src} caption="Dr Cindy Robertson and Dr Anca Doloc-Mihu with the winners Rashun McLaurin, David Jones, and Caleb Polk." /> 

TAP students, David Jones, Caleb Polk, and Rashun McLaurin, won the third place in the 
[Student Research Contest](https://ccsc.org/southeastern/papercontest.php?year=2026) with their
outreach project titled "mBot Tug of War: Python Programming with mBots ToW Game". 

<Photo src={awardPhoto2.src} caption="Rashun McLaurin, David Jones, and Caleb Polk receiving the award." /> 

Another TAP team, composed of Joshua Zepeda, Myran Fonrose, and Jamaal Simmons, was also among the top five finalists 
after the poster session with their project titled "Become a Master of Prompt Engineering". 
Both teams were invited to present their projects in front of the conference audience.
