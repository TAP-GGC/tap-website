# TAP Students and Faculty Presented Research at SITE Conference

<Photo src={site_group.src} caption="Students and faculty in front of conference banner"/> 

TAP students and faculty participated at the 36th annual conference of the [Society for Information Technology and Teacher Education](https://site.aace.org/conf/) (SITE 2025) in Orlando, Florida. 
The conference was held from March 17-21, 2025. Research projects of five TAP student teams were presented as posters and a faculty team presented 
a workshop on "Assessing Creativity in a Technology Outreach Service-Learning Course".
