# TAP  Presentations and Awards at ACM SIGCITE 2026 Conference

TAP participated in the [27th Annual ACM Conference on Cybersecurity and Information Technology Education (ACM SIGCITE 2026)](https://sites.google.com/view/acmsigcite2026conference/home?authuser=0) 
Conference during November 12-14, 2026 in Wilmington, North Carolina. 

<Photo src={tapPhoto.src} caption="TAP faculty members Anca Doloc-Mihu and Cengiz Gunay with TAP students David Jones and Caleb Polk" /> 

The outreach project results of a team of three TAP students, David Jones, Caleb Polk, and Rashun McLaurin, won the best student poster award. 
Their project was titled "mBot Tug of War: Python Programming with mBots ToW Game". The presenting students were David Jones and Caleb Polk, 
who were awarded volunteer positions by the conference organizers.

<Photo src={studentPhoto.src} caption="David Jones and Caleb Polk receiving the award for the team." /> 

TAP faculty member Cengiz Gunay gave a talk titled "An Online Repository of Technology Outreach Projects" about the TAP website's software architecture and impact.

<Photo src={gunayPhoto.src} caption="Cengiz Gunay presenting." /> 

TAP faculty members Xin Xu and Wei Jin also presented their research paper.
