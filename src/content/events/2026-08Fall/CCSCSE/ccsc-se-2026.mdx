# TAP  Presentations and Awards at CCSC:SE 2026

TAP participated in the [2026 Annual CCSC:Southeastern Conference 
(CCSC:SE 2026)](https://ccsc.org/southeastern/conference.php?year=2026) 
that took place during October 23-24, 2026 at East Tennessee State University in Johnson City, Tennessee.

<Photo src={groupPhoto.src} caption="TAP faculty and students at CCSC:SE" /> 

The full set of TAP faculty and the whole cohort of the Fall 2026 TAP course students took part in the conference, presenting their outreach projects. 
Some of Spring 2026 cohort students also presented their outreach projects. 

<Photo src={studentPhoto.src} caption="TAP students at CCSC:SE" /> 

Two TAP student teams were ranked in the top 5 in the student poster competition and were invited to give presentations of their work to the conference attendees. 
The team composed of TAP students David Jones and Caleb Polk won the third place for their project titled "mBot Tug of War: Python Programming with mBots ToW Game".
